Local quality/speed are measured separately from cloud quality/speed. No additional accounts, paid tiers or provider-key rotation were used to evade limits.

## Coverage-first implementation

Editor policy v1 routes any uncertainty, partial camera record, duration margin under 1s, question ending or backup-only judgment to editor review if otherwise passing. A missing cliffhanger is rejected. A bounded alternate audience model uses the same frozen prompt/schema; its scores do not enter the primary ranking. Full queue overlap and new model output fields are deferred.
