Kuku Promo Finder

Find 20–60 second promo moments in timed screenplays, with episode timestamps, opening hooks, unresolved endings and cut instructions.

  1. Upload a ZIP, episode folder or individual timed scripts.
  2. Select episodes and start Groq cloud processing.
  3. Review source-checked cuts and download the cut sheet or CSV.

The interactive upload tool requires JavaScript. A free-host cold start can take about a minute; allow it to load, then refresh if needed.

No company scripts or previous results are preloaded. Uploaded projects belong to the visitor’s session.
